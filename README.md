## What it does

Attrition Risk Radar combines engagement signals, tenure patterns, comp ratios, and workload trends to flag flight risk months early — and gives managers concrete retention playbooks per person.

## Key features

- **Risk scoring** — per-employee flight risk with top contributing factors.
- **Privacy-first design** — aggregate signals, no message content reading.
- **Retention playbooks** — comp review, role change, recognition — matched to cause.
- **Org heatmap** — risk concentration by team, manager, and location.
- **Regrettable-loss tracking** — measure saves, not just exits.

## Who it's for

People leaders and execs who lose key talent they didn't know was unhappy.
